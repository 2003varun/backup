SERVICE AREA 01 05 groups

Data entry services

Most teams don't lack data — they lack time to enter it correctly, twice-check it, and keep it current while everything else on their plate keeps moving. A missed field in a customer record, a mistyped SKU, an order form that sat in someone's inbox for three days: none of it looks urgent until a customer or auditor finds it first.

We take that work off your team's plate and run it as a discipline, not a favour squeezed between other jobs: the same records, entered and checked the same way, every time, so your systems can be trusted without someone re-verifying them. Our data entry team is based in Bangalore and serves clients across India, the Gulf, Europe and North America.

Start a free trial project

What this covers, in practice

Data input & digitization

Physical documents, PDFs, scans and handwritten records go into Excel, databases, CRM and ERP the same way every time, not however whoever is free that day happens to do it. We map each source format to your target fields before a single record moves, so the structure holds even when the volume spikes. The result is a system you can query and trust, instead of one your team quietly double-checks before relying on.

Database maintenance

Customer, supplier, employee and product records drift out of sync the moment more than one person or system touches them. We run scheduled updates, cleansing and duplicate removal so the same person doesn't appear three times under three spellings, and a supplier's changed address doesn't sit stale for months. It's unglamorous work, but it's the difference between a database people trust and one they route around.

E-commerce catalog management

A listing with the wrong price, a missing spec, or an image that doesn't match the product costs you a sale and a support ticket before anyone internally notices. We keep product information, SKUs, descriptions, pricing and image metadata consistent across every platform you sell on, checked against source data rather than copied and hoped for. Customers see the same accurate listing everywhere, and your catalog stops being a source of returns.

Forms & order processing

Online forms, invoices and purchase orders often land in a mix of formats and none of them match what your system expects. We standardize each one before it enters your workflow, catching the missing field or mismatched total at the door instead of three steps downstream. Your order pipeline moves at the speed customers expect, without someone manually reconciling exceptions at the end of the day.

Data verification & auditing

Bad data rarely announces itself; it just sits there until a customer complaint or a failed audit forces the question. We cross-check records against primary sources on a schedule, flagging gaps and errors before they compound into something expensive. You get a running account of how clean your data actually is, not a guess based on how long it's been since anyone looked.

Back-office operations

Claims sitting in a queue, invoices waiting on someone to key them in, stock counts that don't match what's actually on the shelf — this is the work that keeps a business running but never shows up in anyone's job title. When it falls behind, it doesn't announce itself; it just quietly costs you a payment cycle, a customer's patience, or an audit finding.

We run this as a standing operation rather than a backlog someone gets to eventually — documented process, a named team, and turnaround times you can actually plan around.

What this covers, in practice

Document & file management

Records, contracts, forms and operational files pile up fast when nobody owns where they live. We organize, categorize, index and archive them so a document can be found in seconds instead of a search that eats half an afternoon. When an auditor or a customer asks for something from eighteen months ago, the answer is a lookup, not a scramble.

Claims & billing processing

A claim stuck in review or an invoice waiting to be keyed in isn't just paperwork — it's cash flow and a customer relationship both on hold. We handle claims and reimbursement review, invoice preparation, and accounts payable and receivable support on a schedule you can rely on. Payments move, disputes get caught early, and your finance team spends its time on judgment calls instead of data entry.

HR & payroll administration

Employee records, attendance and onboarding documentation are the kind of detail that seems minor until a payroll run goes wrong or a new hire's paperwork is missing on day one. We keep these records current and payroll-related updates accurate, so the people side of your business runs without the fire drills. Your HR team gets to focus on people, not chasing forms.

Inventory & supply chain support

Stock records that don't match the shelf, or a shipment nobody's tracking, turn into a stockout or a customer promise you can't keep. We maintain stock records, order and shipment tracking, supplier data and delivery documentation so what your system says matches what's actually there. You plan against numbers you can trust, not a count from three weeks ago.

Reporting & transcription

Someone on your team is likely spending hours turning calls, meetings or raw numbers into a report that a manager reads once. We produce routine operational and management reports, plus audio and video transcription, formatted the way your business actually uses them. That's time your team gets back for work only they can do.

Order & administrative support

An order that stalls between your customer, your supplier and your own team usually stalls because nobody's clearly responsible for moving it forward. We track customer and supplier orders and order status, and coordinate between internal teams and external stakeholders so nothing sits waiting on a handoff. Orders move at the pace your customers expect, end to end.

Application support activities

A locked-out user, a stalled password reset, a system that's slow and nobody's sure why — small on their own, but they pile up fast when there's no one dedicated to catching them before they become a bigger outage or a frustrated customer. Your engineering team shouldn't be the ones fielding it.

We sit between your users and your systems: watching, responding, and escalating what genuinely needs your engineers' attention, so the rest never reaches them.

What this covers, in practice

Level 1 & Level 2 technical support

A user stuck on a login screen or confused by a feature doesn't want a ticket number, they want to keep working. We handle user queries, software and interface troubleshooting, and day-to-day feature questions directly, so most issues never travel further than the first person who hears about them. Your team gets time back; your users get an answer instead of a queue.

User access & identity management

A locked-out account or a new hire waiting on the right permissions is a small thing that still stops someone's day cold. We manage account creation and provisioning, password resets, unlocks, roles and application permissions, so access issues get resolved in minutes rather than a support ticket sitting overnight. Nobody's blocked from their own tools longer than they have to be.

System monitoring & maintenance

A system that's quietly slowing down usually gives warning signs long before it fails outright — if someone's actually watching. We run availability and performance monitoring, log checks and routine application health checks so degradation gets caught while it's still an easy fix. You hear about problems from us, not from your customers.

Bug triage & escalation

Not every reported issue needs an engineer, but figuring out which ones do shouldn't fall on the engineer either. We document and reproduce reported issues, categorize and prioritize them, and escalate to L3 or engineering only what genuinely needs that level of attention. Your development team's time goes to real problems, not triage.

Patch & version deployment support

Every update carries some risk of breaking something that was working fine yesterday. We handle updates and version changes, pre-deployment testing, and configuration changes and patch implementation with that risk in mind, catching conflicts before they reach production. Deployments become routine instead of a reason to hold your breath.

Incident & service request management

An incident that isn't tracked properly tends to repeat, because nobody captured what actually fixed it last time. We log and track incidents and requests through to resolution, and build that knowledge into support and knowledge-base documentation as we go. The next similar issue gets resolved faster because the last one wasn't wasted.
